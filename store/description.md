# Skeleton Watch: store text

## Short description (`--description`)

A see-through mechanical movement: gears turn behind the hands and the balance ticks.

## Full description

Look straight through the dial into the movement. Gold wheels sit between
polished bridges set with ruby jewels and blued screws, the barrel waits under
its sunburst ratchet wheel at 12, and at 7 o'clock the balance wheel rests on
its blued hairspring.

Flick your wrist and it comes alive. The balance swings back and forth, the
hairspring breathes, the pallet fork snaps from side to side, and at every
beat the escape wheel jumps half a tooth and the whole gear train ticks
forward with it, right behind the hands.

- A full movement: barrel, centre, third and fourth wheels, motion works,
  escape wheel, pallet fork and balance, with the wheels meshing tooth for
  tooth
- The balance wheel ticks, with a breathing hairspring and timing screws
- A power reserve dial at 8 o'clock shows your battery
- Faceted dauphine hands, gold batons and a minute track
- Five finishes: Classic (gold wheels, rhodium bridges), Gold, Steel (with a
  navy chapter ring), Rose gold and Black
- Three hand colours: polished white, blued steel and gold
- Flick your wrist (or tap the screen on Round 2) to set it ticking

Made for the round watches: Pebble Round 2 and Pebble Time Round.

Battery: by default the movement runs for a couple of seconds each minute and
for 12 seconds after a flick, then sits still. You can set it to tick all the
time (uses much more battery) or never.
